OFFICIAL HIGE DANDISM

Formed in 2012, the group is nicknamed "HIGEDAN". The band name reflects their desire to continue making music that excites everyone, even when they reach an age where beards suit them. In April 2015, they released their first mini album, "Love and peace wa kimi no naka," and made their debut. In April 2018, they made their major debut with their first major single, "No doubt." His music has roots in a variety of genres, including black music, and continues to garner support from all generations.
