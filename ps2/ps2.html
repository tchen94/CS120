My Trip to Japan

I went to Japan back in 2024 for the first time. Overall, it was a great experience and an awesome trip, but the only complaint I had was how humid it was. Going to Japan during monsoon season wasn't the best idea, but regardless, it was a good time. Highly recommend going to experience the ryokan and the food (if you're a big fan of sushi, you're at the right place). Take a trip down to Osaka, and you'll be able to experience one of my favorite cuisines in all of Japan. 100% recommend going to Japan, and I hope to go back again in the future!
